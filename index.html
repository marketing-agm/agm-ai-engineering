Who We Are

|In Brief

AGM’s internal software development team designs and builds custom applications for needs that commercial software cannot address cost-effectively. Each solution is developed to reflect how AGM operates. We deliver a focused initial version, place it in the hands of the teams who depend on it, and expand it according to demonstrated use.

Operating Principles

|Stack · Philosophy · Governance
The Stack
Modern Cloud
Serverless hosting, AI-assisted development, and version-controlled code in GitHub enable a small team to deliver at the pace of a much larger one.
The Philosophy
AI Builds the Tools, Not the Answers
We do not address problems by delegating the work to AI. We use AI to build software that reduces friction, saves time, and improves accuracy. These purpose-built tools are delivered in small increments, and subsequent development is guided by actual use.
Governance
Secure by Default
Zero Trust single sign-on, centrally managed credentials, and a complete audit trail. Our AI plan does not train on company data.
Vendor Risk
Portable by Design
Most tools operate without AI at runtime. The components that use AI rely on a re-pointable API, and all data remains available for export at any time.

Why Development Takes Time

|The Work Behind a Feature

A completed feature represents only the visible portion of the work. The majority of the effort precedes the release of any code.

  1. Product design decisions: determining what to build and what to exclude
  2. Feature analysis: accounting for edge cases and real-world workflows
  3. Systems integration: connecting AppFolio, GitHub, Cloudflare, and additional services
  4. Deployment and security configuration: Cloudflare settings, access control, and domains
  5. End-to-end testing: completed before any team relies on the tool
